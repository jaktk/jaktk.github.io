Professional Summary

Engineer and researcher with a PhD in cryogenics, alumnus of two EU H2020 programmes and the Marie Skłodowska-Curie network. Several years of R&D on scientific instruments and optimization techniques, applied to large-scale projects for the Future Circular Collider and the European Spallation Source in collaboration with NIST and Air Liquide. Now building low-cost instrumentation and appropriate technology for global health engineering at ETH Zürich.
